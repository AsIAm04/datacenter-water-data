# Data center concentrations (HUC8 subbasins)
Inputs from the 2026-11-21 study update: pool.csv (20 subbasins with the most mapped atlas sites: atlas site count, states, counties, building footprint, modeled local runoff), record_site.csv (atlas record to site to HUC8), pool_aqueduct.csv (WRI Aqueduct 4.0 baseline values joined by location, HydroBASINS level 6), pool_usdm.csv (U.S. Drought Monitor summaries).
The map layer (data/concentrations_map.json, built by scripts/prep_concentrations.py) outlines the nine subbasins flagged case=True in pool_usdm.csv. That flag is the only place the files define the nine, so confirm the list. Outlines come from the simplified WBD HUC8 shapes used for the HUC8 layer.
Label: geographic concentration, not a verified shared supply. Atlas is OpenStreetMap-derived and incomplete.
Checks: pool.csv site counts equal the number of distinct sites per HUC8 in record_site.csv for 15 of 20 subbasins and are one lower for five (Middle Potomac-Anacostia-Occoquan, Middle Potomac-Catoctin, Des Plaines, Upper Scioto, Big Papillion-Mosquito), where a site touches two subbasins and pool.csv counts it once. Assigning each site to its most frequent HUC8 reproduces 18 of the 20 counts (Des Plaines gives 20 against 21 and Upper Chattahoochee 11 against 12), so the exact assignment rule is not confirmed. The page uses the pool.csv counts.
Not used on the page: the Drought Monitor columns, because their definitions are not documented in the files. Aqueduct needs the 261 MB WRI geodatabase for a map-wide layer and was computed for these 20 only.
