
- `data/huc8_modeled_map.json`: map data for the modeled-subbasin layer and county panel. `h` = HUC8 to [name, Siddik 2018 footprint Mgal/yr (PCA method), min and max of six attribution methods, USGS modeled public supply, irrigation and thermoelectric withdrawals Mgal/d for 2015 and 2020, x, y]; `c` = county to [up to 4 overlapping HUC8 by area, count of HUC8 with at least 2% of their area in the county]. Built by scripts/prep_modeled_huc8.py.
- `data/supply/nwdc_wu_huc8_annual_mgd_2000_2020.csv`: USGS National Water Availability Assessment Data Companion wide files (public supply pswdtot, crop irrigation irrwdtot, thermoelectric fresh tewdftot), monthly HUC12 values converted to day-weighted annual mean Mgal/d and summed to HUC8. Thermoelectric starts 2008.
- `data/usgs_2020_reconciliation_national.csv` and `data/usgs_2020_reconciliation_county.csv`: reconciliation test of USGS modeled withdrawals against the 1985 to 2015 county compilations.

Siddik 2018 footprint semantics (checked against the paper, Section 2.3 and 3.1): consumptive water; direct cooling water is assigned to the watershed where the supplying utility withdraws, indirect electricity water to the watershed of the power plant; hydropower reservoir evaporation is assigned to the dam primary purpose. National total 5.13e8 m3 for 2018, about three-fourths indirect. The HUC8 table sums to 5.07e8 m3 (PCA method; 4.4e8 to 5.8e8 across the six methods). Large values sit at hydropower reservoirs (Lower Lake Powell, Lower Sabine, Pend Oreille).

USGS 2020 reconciliation result: the 2020 modeled estimates cannot be joined to the compiled county series. National modeled/compiled ratios in the same year run 0.72 to 0.90 for public supply and irrigation and 0.83 to 0.85 for thermoelectric (compiled totals also include Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico, and compiled irrigation includes golf courses). With HUC8 volumes allocated to counties by area (crude), county agreement is weak: Spearman 0.56 public supply, 0.72 irrigation, 0.12 to 0.15 thermoelectric; within a factor of 2 for 56%, 48% and 25% of counties. The Data Companion does not publish county aggregates, and 2020 livestock and aquaculture are not available. Decision: 2015 and earlier stay the county series; 2020 appears only as a separately labeled modeled HUC8 column in the county panel.
